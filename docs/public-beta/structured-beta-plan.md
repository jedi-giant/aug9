# Aug9 structured public-beta plan

## Objective

Validate that Aug9 helps people complete real Singapore lifestyle tasks across food, events, playgrounds, weather and transport while maintaining context between turns.

## Cohort

Recruit 20–30 participants across parents, food explorers, event seekers, residents in different regions, and visitors. Run the study for 7–10 days. Do not collect sensitive personal data.

## Required journeys

Each participant completes at least five journeys, including:

1. Find an indoor or age-appropriate playground in a named neighbourhood.
2. Continue with “find somewhere to eat nearby” without repeating the location.
3. Find three dinner options near a specific address.
4. Find current or upcoming events, then open one event.
5. Ask about weather and a practical transport option.

Participants should also attempt two natural tasks of their own.

## What to capture

- Task completion and time to useful answer.
- Response-level and card-level feedback.
- Negative reason: inaccurate, outdated, too far, lost context or not relevant.
- Whether an action was opened.
- A short optional comment after a failed journey.

## Launch gates

- At least 80% of scripted journeys reach a useful answer.
- Fewer than 10% of follow-up turns are marked “lost context”.
- No critical privacy, authentication or cross-user context defect.
- Production smoke tests pass for seven consecutive days.
- Daily import health has no unexplained failure or stale source.

The beta owner reviews metrics every two days and pauses recruitment for any critical incident.
