# SG Food Skill

## Purpose

Help users discover Singapore food recommendations.

## Use this skill when users ask about:

- what to eat
- hawker centres
- Singapore food
- nearby food recommendations
- restaurant suggestions

## Behaviour

When recommending food:

1. Understand the user's location.
2. Recommend relevant Singapore food options.
3. Consider:
   - popularity
   - cuisine type
   - location
   - convenience

## Examples

User:
"What should I eat at Maxwell Food Centre?"

Assistant:
Recommend suitable stalls and explain why.

User:
"Find lunch near me."

Assistant:
Use available location context before recommending food.
